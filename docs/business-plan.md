# Business plan (one page)

**Product.** HearthUI — a new-tab launcher for Chrome, live in the Chrome Web Store ([INSTALLS] users, [RATING]★). Solana adds wallet sign-in, USDC payments and an on-chain theme marketplace (`hearth_pass`).

**Problem.** The new tab is the most-viewed screen on the desktop, yet creators who design themes, icon packs and wallpapers for it have no simple way to sell them: a payment backend, a processor account, tax/refund handling and a supported country are all prerequisites.

**Solution.** Creators list a theme on-chain; buyers pay in USDC from Phantom; the program splits revenue 95% creator / 5% HearthUI atomically and issues a receipt PDA that the extension verifies from public RPC. No server holds entitlements; sales settle instantly and globally.

**Customers.** (1) HearthUI users who personalise their workspace; (2) independent designers and Solana-native artists; (3) communities/brands wanting a branded new tab for members (later).

**Revenue model.**
- 5% marketplace take rate on every theme/icon/pass sale (on-chain, transparent).
- Supporter tier: 4.99 USDC one-off (direct to escrow) — unlocks supporter themes.
- Later: featured listings for creators; branded community tabs (B2B).

**Market (TAM/SAM/SOM — owner to verify sources).** Chrome: ~3B+ users [SOURCE]. New-tab/start-page extensions: [N] million weekly users across top listings [SOURCE]. SAM: users of customization extensions willing to pay ≈ [X]%. SOM year 1: [INSTALLS] × [conversion %] × [avg spend] = [$].

**Traction.** Live product, [N] releases since [DATE], [INSTALLS] installs, [WAU] weekly users, [RATING]★ — [owner fills; do not estimate].

**Go-to-market.** Existing users first (opt-in Solana module) → seed 5–10 creators with launch themes → Blinks so creators sell from X posts → Solana ecosystem partnerships (wallet, NFT/art communities).

**Competition.** Momentum, Bonjourr, Infinity New Tab, Tabliss: subscriptions or free, no creator payouts. Web3 wallets: no customization surface. HearthUI is the only new tab with a trustless creator economy.

**Costs & milestones.** Solo founder + contract help. M1 (Nov): mainnet program after review. M2 (Dec): creator self-serve upload. M3 (Q1): Blinks + 25 creators. Funding ask: [AMOUNT] for audit, creator grants and 6 months of runway.

**Risks.** Chrome Web Store policy on crypto features → keep the store build crypto-free until approved, ship as opt-in; wallet UX → bridge page + Phantom; low creator supply → seed grants.
